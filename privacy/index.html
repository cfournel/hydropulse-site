Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

HydroPulse estimates your hydration needs from data that stays on your watch. There is no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

Summary

We do not sell data, run ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Anonymous weather lookups are the only data this app sends off-device — coordinates only, with no account or device identifier attached.

What we do not do

  • No account creation, no login, no user identifiers.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No backend of ours — the only network calls are anonymous weather lookups.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Health data

Heart rate and activity data are sensitive under Google Play’s health and fitness data policies. HydroPulse uses this data solely to compute the in-app hydration estimate, does not transmit raw heart-rate or activity data anywhere, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

Permissions requested, and why

  • BODY_SENSORS — read heart rate for the exertion component of the model.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — detect steps and exercise sessions.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — look up local weather. Precise location is never requested.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — deliver hydration reminders.

All four are optional. Declined, the app keeps working with sensible defaults for the components it can no longer sense.

Data retention and deletion

All personal data — profile, water log, ledger — lives in local app storage on your watch. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no server-side account or profile to delete, because none exists.

Children’s privacy

HydroPulse is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

Material changes are reflected here with an updated date above, and shipped alongside an app release note.
